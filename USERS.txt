GETTING STARTED (FIRST-TIME RUN):
------------------------------------------------------------------------
When launching the application on a fresh installation with no existing
database:
1. The system automatically launches the "First-Time Setup" dialog.
2. Enter your desired Store Manager username, full name, and a secure
   password (minimum 8 characters, at least one letter and one number;
   e.g. Username: Ajmal, Password: ajmal@123).
3. Once created, sign in with your credentials to access the Dashboard.
4. From the "Staff & Users" tab, the Store Manager can register
   additional Sales Assistant or Store Manager accounts.

SYSTEM ROLES & ACCESS CONTROL PRIVILEGES:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Store Manager (Full System Privileges):
   - Model Class: model.StoreManager (extends model.User)
   - Privileges: Full Inventory CRUD (Add, Edit, Delete Products),
                 Staff Management (Create & Delete Users),
                 Stock In / Stock Out (Restocking & POS Checkout),
                 Low Stock Reorder Alerts & Quick Restock,
                 CSV Exports for Inventory and Stock Movement Audit Trail.

2. Sales Assistant (Operational Privileges):
   - Model Class: model.SalesAssistant (extends model.User)
   - Privileges: Inventory Browsing & Multi-Criteria Search,
                 Stock In (Supplier Deliveries),
                 Point of Sale (Customer Checkout & Receipt Generation),
                 Low Stock Reorder Alerts.
   - Prohibited Operations: Cannot delete products, cannot create or
     delete staff accounts. Enforced at both the UI and Service layer.

SECURITY & RELIABILITY HIGHLIGHTS:
========================================================================
• Cryptographic Password Security: PBKDF2 with HMAC-SHA256, unique per-user
  cryptographic random salt (16 bytes), and 65,536 iterations.
• Zero Hardcoded Credentials: No passwords or secret keys in source code.
• Constant-Time Hash Verification: MessageDigest.isEqual defends against
  timing side-channel attacks.
• Account Lockout Defense: 5 consecutive failed login attempts trigger a
  15-minute temporary lockout.
• Deserialization Whitelist: JEP 290 class filtering prevents arbitrary
  class instantiation during deserialization.
• Atomic File Persistence: Data is written to temporary files and atomically
  moved over the target, preventing file corruption on unexpected crashes.
• Financial Precision: All monetary transactions and inventory valuations
  use java.math.BigDecimal (no floating-point rounding errors).
• CSV Formula Injection Defense: Exported cells starting with =, +, -, @
  are automatically escaped.
